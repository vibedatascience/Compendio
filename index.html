Compendio

The complete record of Europe's top club competition. Every match of every season since 1956 — with goalscorers, venues, lineups, standings, brackets, and the best publicly listed highlight video found for each.

6,039 matches across 71 seasons, 1955/56 to 2025/26. Match data, goalscorers, venues, and lineups from UEFA's public match feed; crests from UEFA's image service. Videos are embedded from their original YouTube uploaders — nothing is hosted or copied here, and a blank player means the uploader removed or restricted it. Matches without an indexed video link to a targeted search and the official UEFA.com match page.
