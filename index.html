Compendio

The complete record of Europe's top club competition. Every fixture of every season — league phase, groups, playoffs, knockouts, finals — each linked to the best publicly listed highlight video found for it. Matches without an indexed video yet link straight to a targeted search.

1,997 matches across 15 modern seasons plus 56 classic finals back to 1956. Videos are embedded from their original YouTube uploaders; nothing is hosted or copied here, and a blank player means the uploader removed or restricted it. Fixture data: openfootball (public domain).
